HIV

What you need to know.

HIV is a virus that weakens your body's defence system. With treatment, you can live a long, healthy life — and you cannot pass it on.

1 Three things to know

Start here.

  • HIV is treatable. Treatment is free at every public clinic in South Africa.
  • If treatment makes your viral load undetectable, you cannot pass HIV to anyone — even without a condom. (U=U)
  • Anyone can have HIV. Testing is the only way to know. There is no shame in testing.
2 Signs to look for

Early signs.

Many people have no symptoms for years. Early signs — within weeks of infection — can include:

🌡️
Fever
😴
Tiredness
💪
Body aches
🗣️
Sore throat
🔴
Skin rash
👤
Swollen glands

These signs can also come from other illnesses. Only a test can confirm HIV.

3 How it spreads

Know the facts.

HIV IS passed through
  • Unprotected sex (vaginal, anal, or oral)
  • Sharing needles or syringes
  • Mother to baby (preventable with treatment)
HIV is NOT passed through
  • Hugging, kissing, sharing food or drinks
  • Toilet seats or swimming pools
  • Mosquito or insect bites
4 Myths vs Facts

Don't believe the lies.

MYTH
HIV is a death sentence.
FACT
With treatment, people with HIV live a normal lifespan. Treatment is free.
MYTH
You can tell who has HIV by looking at them.
FACT
You cannot. Most people with HIV look completely healthy. Only a test reveals HIV.
5 Take action

Get tested.

  • Free testing at any public clinic — walk in any day.
  • Home test kits are at most pharmacies.
  • Test as often as you want. No appointment, no judgement.
  • If positive, treatment starts the same day at any clinic.
6 Prevention

Stay HIV-free.

  • C
    Condoms
    Free at every public clinic. Carry them.
  • P
    PrEP
    A daily pill that prevents HIV. Free at clinics.
  • E
    PEP
    Emergency pills within 72 hours of possible exposure.
  • S
    Treat other STIs
    They make HIV easier to catch and pass on.
